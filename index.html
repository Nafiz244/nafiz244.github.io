About

I work in AI in Healthcare, Ai, Computer Vision, and NLP. My main goal is to build reliable and explainable systems for healthcare. Current work spans diabetic retinopathy screening from fundus images, multi-type diabetes detection (Type 1, Type 2, Gestational), mental-health analytics for university students, and agentic AI workflows for decision support. I focus on data quality, fairness, calibration, and deployment constraints so models translate into real clinical value. Moreover, I love to do research, especially in healthare

Running Projects

Mental Health Detection among University Students

Survey + passive signals; interpretable risk stratification; privacy-preserving analytics; referral triage.

  • Models: gradient boosting, calibrated logistic, transformers (text)
  • Focus: bias checks, stability, human-in-the-loop

Diabetic Retinopathy Detection (Fundus Images)

Quality-aware grading; class imbalance handling; Grad-CAM/SHAP overlays for clinical review.

  • Pipelines: preprocessing, lesion cues, multi-head classification
  • Targets: sensitivity ≥90% at fixed specificity

Diabetes Detection — Type 1, Type 2, Gestational

Tabular + imaging fusion; calibrated risk; pathway-aware features; external validation across cohorts.

  • Metrics: AUROC, AUPRC, ECE, Brier score
  • Explainability: global + local (SHAP), counterfactuals

Agentic AI in Healthcare

Task-orchestrated agents for data cleaning, literature triage, coding assistance, and decision support with safety rails.

  • Guardrails: tool permissions, uncertainty prompts, audit logs
  • Outcomes: time-to-insight, error-rate reduction
